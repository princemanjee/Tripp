# Specification Input: FSMA 204 Traceability Compliance for Small Food Businesses

A test input for the template. Planted defects the emission must surface rather than resolve: one internal contradiction ("ridiculously simple" against a nine-feature MVP, exercising the contradiction rule), one missing input (the FDA Key Data Elements and Food Traceability List definitions are referenced but not supplied), one ambiguity ("relevant products" is undefined), and two unquantified requirements ("produce those records rapidly" and the "compliance-readiness score" carry no number and no method).

## What we want

A ridiculously simple compliance system for small food manufacturers, distributors, importers, wholesalers, and specialty retailers that need to maintain FDA Food Traceability Rule records.

Do not build another giant food ERP. Build: upload your invoices, shipment records, and receiving documents, and we turn them into an FDA-ready traceability record.

## Features

- Import CSVs, PDFs, and spreadsheets.
- Identify relevant products.
- Track lot and batch information.
- Map Critical Tracking Events.
- Flag missing Key Data Elements.
- Maintain a searchable audit trail.
- Generate the sortable spreadsheet the FDA may request.
- Produce a compliance-readiness score.
- Send automated reminders to suppliers and customers for missing information.

## Users

- Small Business Operator: uploads documents, reads the readiness score, chases gaps.
- Supplier or Customer Contact: receives reminder requests for missing information.
- FDA Auditor (indirect): receives the sortable spreadsheet when records are requested.

## Notes

- The FDA rule requires covered businesses to maintain specific Key Data Elements associated with Critical Tracking Events and be able to produce those records rapidly when requested.
- The original January 20, 2026 compliance date has effectively been pushed to July 20, 2028. Treat this as a preparation window, not a reason to defer.
- Customers are small; assume no IT department and no data team.
